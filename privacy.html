Privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

Jarvis is a personal assistant designed, built and owned by Nazeem Mohamed. It runs on its owner's own Windows PC and is used only by its owner. This page explains what it does with Google account data.

What Jarvis accesses

  • Gmail: new messages, so it can sort them into labels, flag urgent ones, and save reply drafts when asked. It does not send, delete or archive email.
  • Google Calendar: events (read-only), so it can say what's on. It cannot change or delete them.

Access is only to the Google account its owner connects, and the owner can disconnect it at any time from their Google account permissions.

Where the data goes

  • Sign-in tokens and email labels/notes are stored on the owner's own PC.
  • To sort email and write summaries, the text of messages is sent to Google's Gemini AI service for processing, using the owner's own Gemini key.
  • Summaries and alerts are sent to the owner's own phone (Telegram and the owner's private phone app).

Data is never sold, shared with anyone else, used for advertising, or used to train AI models by Jarvis.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

The owner can delete everything Jarvis keeps from its Privacy Dashboard, and can remove its access to Google at any time from their Google account.
