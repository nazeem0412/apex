/ Desktop companion

Meet Apex, in person

Apex lives on the desktop as a small animated companion. He walks along the taskbar, sits on the edge of the window you're working in, and is one click away from everything Apex can do. Double-click him and the full interface opens. Watch him in action:

Chat

Click to talk

One click opens a chat box above his head; replies appear in his speech bubble, with buttons when there's a next step.

Drop

Drop files on him

Drag a document, picture or folder onto him and he offers the right actions: summarise it, save it to memory, send it to your phone.

Wheel

Quick-action wheel

Rest the mouse on him and eight buttons fan out: Talk, Music, Today, Trick, Game, Focus, Fetch and Settings.

Focus

Focus buddy

Start a focus session and he holds every nudge until the timer ends, then celebrates with you.

Notice

Spots trouble

When an error box or a frozen app appears, he flinches and offers to look at your screen and help.

Buddy

Work buddy

Lunch, break and water reminders, a Monday pep talk, a Friday wrap-up, and a joke or tip now and then, never during a meeting or a call.

Mood

Two personalities

Professional during work hours, Fun after them, or pick one yourself. Same tools and memory, different tone.

Tour

Live showcase

Ask for the grand tour and he demonstrates his tools for real on screen, then tidies up everything he opened.

He also plays games, does tricks, plays fetch, grooves when music plays, controls YouTube Music, and his eyes follow your mouse. Every option, for him and for all of Apex, sits on one Settings page.

/ Interface

One core, four moods

The desktop interface changes colour and motion with what Apex is doing, so its state reads at a glance from across the room.

  1. 01 · Blue

    Idle

    Standing by and ready. The core breathes slowly and the rings drift while Apex waits for the next request.

  2. 02 · Amber

    Thinking

    Working on a request. Everything turns amber, the rings speed up, and the tool being used shows under the core.

  3. 03 · Blue, live

    Speaking

    Answering out loud. The core pulses with the voice and the voice meter moves with every word.

  4. 04 · Grey

    Held

    Kill switch engaged. Every background action is paused and the whole interface goes grey until it is released.

Red appears only when the AI service is rate-limited.

/ Security and privacy

The owner stays in control

Apex runs on its owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Asks before acting

Anything that changes files, sends, clicks or deletes needs approval first.

Approval password

The most sensitive actions need a password only the owner knows, never spoken aloud.

Kill switch

A single command pauses every background action instantly.

Privacy dashboard

See everything Apex keeps, and delete any of it.

Offline backups

Daily, checked backups to a USB drive in the owner's hands.

Undo what it did

Anything done on its own is logged and can be undone, and the riskiest actions always ask.
